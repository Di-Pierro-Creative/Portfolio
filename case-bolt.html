Departure · The challenge

Global consistency without global sameness

A ride in Bangkok, a food delivery in Sofia, a driver sign-up in Nairobi, a business account in Prague — Bolt is a different promise in each, and its trust is intensely local. The brief was never "make an ad." It was to run a continuous creative operation that could hold one recognisable Bolt voice across five verticals and dozens of cultures at once, while still feeling native in every feed.

Two constraints shaped everything: the work had to scale — new markets and launches landed constantly — and it had to stay locally credible, because a tone-deaf ad in a launch market costs more than money.

Each vertical speaks to a different person with a different job-to-be-done. I set the creative strategy across all five, keeping a shared brand language while tuning the message per audience — riders, eaters, drivers, business travellers, and Bolt Plus subscribers.

Stop 2 · The system

Regional masters, localised without reshooting

Volume at this scale only works if you're not reinventing every asset. The model: build a strong regional master concept — often produced via TikTok Creative Exchange — then localise it out to individual markets with local creators, local idiom, and the same underlying structure. One Driver concept could spin out into eight market localisations; a single Rider idea like "Riding is the New Driving" ran natively across Europe, Africa, and Asia.

Concept

One regional master built to travel — structure and hook designed for adaptation from the start.

Localise

Local creator, local language, cultural reference swapped — the architecture stays, the skin changes.

Voice / no voice

Concepts built with and without VO so markets could deploy fast, with or without a voice actor.

QA

Script, translation, and creative reviewed per market so nothing shipped that read as foreign.

Two brands

Same system carried both Bolt and Hopp — the brand name Bolt uses in Canada and select markets.

Repeat

~14 productions a month, sequenced across verticals and markets on a rolling calendar.

Terminus · Outcome

A repeatable engine, not a campaign

The result wasn't a hero film — it was an operating system for creative: a rolling pipeline of ~14 productions a month, a library of reusable concepts, and a localisation model that let Bolt enter or refresh a market without commissioning fresh production each time. Concept, script, creator, translation, and delivery ran as one sequenced flow across every vertical and region.

It's the part of the job most creative reels never show — the ability to run a sprawling, multi-brand, multi-continent account and keep it shipping, on brand, and locally credible, week after week.
