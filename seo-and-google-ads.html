What are SEO, Google Ads and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) earns your place in organic results. Google Ads buys your place at the exact moment of intent. GEO (generative engine optimization) makes your brand legible to AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity, which increasingly answer buyers’ questions directly. We run the three as one system because they serve the same moment: someone asking for exactly what you sell.

Why a system, not pieces

Should SEO and Google Ads be run separately?

They plateau alone. Run together, each de-risks the other: paid search reveals in days which queries convert — those become SEO priorities; organic rankings lower the cost of the same demand ads were buying. Separate vendors never share that data. One system, one feedback loop, on Google and Bing alike.

Frequently asked questions
SEO or Google Ads — which should I start with?

Usually both, at different intensities: Ads validates which queries convert in weeks; SEO then builds durable rankings for the proven ones. Starting with only one leaves data on the table.

How long until SEO shows results?

Technical fixes move in weeks; content rankings typically build over 3–6 months. We report leading indicators monthly so progress is visible before revenue lands.

Do you work with sites you didn’t build?

Yes — we audit, fix, and manage existing sites. If the platform fights conversion, we’ll say so and propose the smallest rebuild that solves it.
