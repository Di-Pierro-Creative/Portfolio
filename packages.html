Questions

Do I have to start with the audit?

For cycles, yes: no cycle starts without Analyze, because that's what points to the leverage point. Kits can be booked directly.

Why are prices “from”?

Because the figure depends on the size of your operation — how many channels, tools and people the system covers. We agree the price in writing after the audit, before any work starts.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok). Packages cover strategy, creative, implementation and reading the results.
