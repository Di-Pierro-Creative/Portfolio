The Di Pierro Loop — Our Methodology
01

Analyze

We map the whole system first — data, funnel, customer voice — and find the leverage point worth attacking.

02

Create

Hypotheses become concepts and systems, built around real motivations and objections.

03

Test

Every test answers a hypothesis from the framework — so every result teaches something.

04

Winners validated with real spend and real metrics before they earn more budget.

05

Proven winners become systems — across channels, markets, and languages.

The loop closes when the system compounds: what customers say and what the data shows feed the next cycle — better ads, faster flows, a sharper system.

Why It Works — Systems Thinking, Applied

Systems thinking is the discipline of seeing a business as one system — parts connected by flows of information — instead of isolated pieces. We use it because growth rarely stalls inside a channel; it stalls in the connections between them. Three principles run the Loop:

Analyze · FlixBus

Find the leverage point

FlixBus was outspent nine to one. Analysis showed reach was unwinnable — but conversion wasn’t. We rebuilt the creative around a six-beat conversion formula: CPA down 67% in 90 days, on a ninth of the incumbent’s spend.

A small change at the right point moves the whole system.

Test → Prove · Ocean Finance

Close the feedback loop

Each cycle’s results became the next cycle’s brief: pain-point hooks won cycle one, so cycle two asked which pain, cycle three which messenger. CPA went from $53 to $6.82 in three cycles.

Outputs that feed back into inputs compound. Plans that don’t, stall.

Scale · Bolt

Build stocks, not one-offs

Winning concepts became a reusable asset: master concepts localized across 29 markets — 160+ productions without restarting the thinking each time.

A system that accumulates gets cheaper every cycle. One-off campaigns start from zero forever.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is systems thinking, and why does a growth studio use it?

Systems thinking is the discipline of seeing a business as one connected system — ads, pages, funnel, support — rather than isolated parts. We use it because growth usually stalls in the connections: the ad promises what the page doesn’t deliver, support hears objections the ads never learn about. The Di Pierro Loop applies it in five stages: Analyze, Create, Test, Prove, Scale.

What is the Di Pierro Loop?

The Di Pierro Loop is our methodology: we Analyze the system to find the leverage point, Create hypotheses, Test them, Prove winners with real metrics, and Scale what works into reusable assets. The loop closes when results compound anywhere in the system — better ads, faster flows, a sharper operation.

What is Di Pierro Creative?

Di Pierro Creative is a growth studio founded in 2019 in São Paulo, working globally in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. We build the systems brands grow on: AI workflows, websites, SEO and Google Ads, dashboards, social media and performance creative, and app growth.
