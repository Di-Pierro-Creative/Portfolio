What is an AI workflow?

An AI workflow is a repeatable process in which AI handles defined steps — reading, tagging, drafting, routing — inside rules designed by a person, with clear handoffs to your team. It is not a chatbot bolted onto a website: it is your existing process, redesigned so the repetitive load runs itself. We build them with systems thinking because most growth problems do not live inside one team — they live in the handoffs between marketing, sales, and support.
